Brief Overview

The Sundarbans is the largest mangrove forest in the world, located in Bangladesh and India. This UNESCO World Heritage Site supports incredible biodiversity including Royal Bengal Tigers, crocodiles, dolphins, birds, and hundreds of plant species. It protects coastal regions from cyclones and maintains ecological balance.

Wildlife

Royal Bengal Tiger

The Sundarbans is famous for the Royal Bengal Tiger, one of the most powerful and endangered animals in the world.

Bird Species

Hundreds of bird species including kingfishers, herons, eagles, and migratory birds live here.

Mangrove Flora

Mangrove Forest

Mangrove trees survive in salty water and protect coastal regions from erosion and cyclones.

Unique Ecosystem

The Sundarbans ecosystem supports marine life, birds, reptiles, and rare plants.
