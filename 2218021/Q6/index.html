Bird of the Day

Common Kingfisher

The Common Kingfisher is one of the most beautiful birds in Bangladesh. It is famous for its bright blue feathers and excellent fishing skills.

Scientific Name: Alcedo atthis

Habitat: Rivers, wetlands, ponds
