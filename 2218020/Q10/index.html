Fuchka Recipe

Preparation Time: 20 Minutes

Ingredients

  • Fuchka shells
  • Potato
  • Chickpeas
  • Tamarind water

Instructions

  1. Boil potatoes.
  2. Mix ingredients.
  3. Fill shells.
  4. Serve with tamarind water.

Chotpoti Recipe

Preparation Time: 25 Minutes

Ingredients

  • Chickpeas
  • Potato

Instructions

  2. Add spices and potato.
  3. Mix properly.
  4. Serve hot.

Jhalmuri Recipe

Preparation Time: 10 Minutes

Ingredients

  • Puffed rice
  • Onion
  • Chili
  • Mustard oil

Instructions

  1. Take puffed rice.
  2. Add onion and chili.

  4. Serve fresh.

Mishti Recipe

Preparation Time: 30 Minutes

Ingredients

  • Milk
  • Sugar
  • Cardamom

Instructions

  1. Boil milk.
  2. Add sugar.
  3. Cook until thick.
  4. Serve cool.

Rosogolla Recipe

Preparation Time: 40 Minutes

Ingredients

  • Milk
  • Sugar syrup
  • Lemon juice

Instructions

  1. Prepare chana from milk.
  2. Make small balls.
  3. Boil in sugar syrup.
  4. Serve chilled.
