Lalbagh Fort

Lalbagh Fort is an incomplete Mughal fort complex in Dhaka, Bangladesh. Built during the 17th century, it stands as a remarkable example of Mughal architecture.

Paharpur

Somapura Mahavihara in Paharpur is one of South Asia’s most important Buddhist monasteries. It was built during the Pala dynasty and is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Mahasthangarh

Mahasthangarh is one of the earliest urban archaeological sites discovered in Bangladesh. It represents the ancient city of Pundranagara.

Shat Gombuj Mosque

The Shat Gombuj Mosque is a UNESCO World Heritage Site located in Bagerhat. It was built during the Bengal Sultanate period and is famous for its many domes.
